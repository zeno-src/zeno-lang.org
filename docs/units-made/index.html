Units made of units: V A is W

A quantity in Zeno carries its unit: 3.3 V, 20 mA, 500 ms. Two quantities of the right kinds now make a third, so Ohm's law, power, energy and periods are written as they are on paper:

let supply = 3.3 V
let draw = 20 mA
let power = supply * draw
print(power)               # 0.066 W
print(power.mW)            # 66 mW
print(supply / draw)       # 165 ohm
print(1 / 2 ms)            # 500 Hz

The reasons for each choice are in chapter 43 of decisions.md.

What makes what

Write Gives For example
voltage * current power 5 V * 2 A is 10 W
current * resistance voltage 2 mA * 4.7 kohm is 9.4 V
power * time energy 5 W * 2 h is 36000 J
voltage / current resistance 5 V / 2 mA is 2500 ohm
voltage / resistance current 12 V / 4.7 kohm is about 0.00255 A
power / voltage, power / current current, voltage 100 W / 230 V is about 0.435 A
energy / time, energy / power power, time 1 kWh / 1 h is 1000 W
a number / time frequency 1 / 2 ms is 500 Hz
a number / frequency time 1 / 1 kHz is 0.001 s
time * frequency a plain number 10 ms * 1 kHz is 10.0

A product works either way round: 20 mA * 3.3 V is also 0.066 W.

The unit of the answer

The answer is in the unit of its kind with no prefix: W, A, V, ohm, J, s or Hz. Change it with a dot, as for any quantity:

let energy = 5 W * 2 h
print(energy)              # 36000 J
print(energy.Wh)           # 10 Wh
let r = 3.3 V / 20 mA
print(r.kohm)              # 0.165 kohm

A function says which unit it gives, and the checker holds it to it:

function watts(v: V, i: mA) -> W {
    return v * i
}
print(watts(5 V, 120 mA))  # 0.6 W

Asking for mW where the answer is in W is an error that says to write .mW.

What is refused

Any pair not in the table, such as 2 m * 3 m or 1 / 2 V, is an error before the program runs, and the message lists what works. Zeno does not invent a unit such as m^2: every unit has a name on the list, so a slip in a sum is still caught.

In a built program

zeno build keeps a quantity a double and knows its unit while compiling, so supply * draw is each side scaled by a number known while building, and one multiplication. Every engine and every target language prints the same digits.

Not yet

Charge (mAh, for battery life), speed (m / s), units a program names itself, and sum of quantities. See 43.6 in decisions.md.

Every journey is halfway done. Forever.

The runner has closed 0 of 1 halves

Zeno of Elea said Achilles can never catch the tortoise: first he must cover half the distance, then half of what is left, without end. It suits a compiler that will always be almost finished. The bar at the top of each page works the same way.
