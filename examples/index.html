Every journey is halfway done. Forever.

The runner has closed 0 of 1 halves

Zeno of Elea said Achilles can never catch the tortoise: first he must cover half the distance, then half of what is left, without end. It suits a compiler that will always be almost finished. The bar at the top of each page works the same way.
