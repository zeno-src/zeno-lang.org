A Keywords

These 37 words are the keywords of Zeno. A keyword is never a name: a variable, function or field cannot be called match or take, and the compiler says so if one is.

Words that are not keywords

A few words have a meaning in one place and are ordinary names everywhere else, because in that place no name could stand:

  • test, before a string at the top of a file, starts a test (Testing).

  • package, needs and at are the words of a zeno.package file (Packages).

  • The names of the built-in types (int, str, list, u8 and the rest), of the units (ms, V) and of the built-in functions (print, len) are names declared by Zeno, which a program may declare again in an inner block.

Words from other languages

These are not Zeno, and each gets a message with the Zeno spelling: def and fn (function), elif (else if), &&, || and ! (and, or, not), and a ; at the end of a line. So do the names other languages give to nothing and to the two truths: None, null, nil, NULL, nullptr and undefined are told that Zeno spells it none, and True and False that it spells them true and false.
